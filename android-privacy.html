Chronolyze for Android — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Chronolyze is a timegrapher for mechanical watches, made by Hakan Kutluay. This policy covers the Android app as published on Google Play.

In short: Chronolyze collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and it doesn't use the internet at all.

The microphone

Chronolyze measures your watch by listening to its ticking through the microphone. The microphone is on only while a measurement is running. Audio is analysed on this phone as it arrives; nothing is uploaded. The app doesn't save the audio. A finished measurement keeps only its results (rate, beat error, amplitude, stability) and the dots of its trace.

You can refuse or withdraw microphone access at any time in Android's settings. Demo mode works without it.

What stays on your phone

  • The watches you add, with their lift angle, beat rate and movement.
  • Your measurement history: each session's results and trace.
  • Your settings.

This data is stored only on your phone. If you have Android backup turned on, Android includes it in your backup to your Google account, and restores it when you set up a new phone. That backup is handled by Android and Google under your Google account; the developer never receives or sees it. You can delete watches and sessions in the app, and uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone.

What isn't there

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • No internet access: the app doesn't request Android's internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere.
  • No location, contacts, photos or other personal information.

Children

Chronolyze isn't directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date. A change that affects your data will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
